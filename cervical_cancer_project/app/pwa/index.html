05 · Scientific safeguards

Keep the interpretation honest

01Cell classes are not cancer diagnoses

The available dataset labels are Dyskeratotic, Koilocytotic, Metaplastic, Parabasal and Superficial-Intermediate.

02Clinical data stays independent

Image and clinical records have no shared patient identifier, so feature-level fusion is not claimed.

03Every demo is synthetic

Demo records exist to preview the product UI and must never be treated as patient information or ground truth.
